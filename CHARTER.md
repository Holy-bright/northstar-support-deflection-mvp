---

## Communication Norms
- Primary channel: team group chat (respond within 2 hours during work hours, 9am–6pm)
- Daily async standup posted by 9:30am: what I did yesterday, what I'm doing today, any blockers
- Decisions affecting the whole team require a thumbs-up from at least 2 other members before proceeding

## Deadlines
- Board status updated **same day** work is done — no batching
- All commits follow the convention: `<type>: <what changed> - <why it matters>`
- No commit message of "wip", "updates", or similar vague labels — PR will be rejected

## Definition of Done (Global)
A task is Done when:
1. Code/doc is committed with a compliant message
2. Board card is moved to **Done** column
3. At least one other member has reviewed or acknowledged the output

## Conflict Resolution
1. Raise the issue in the team channel same day it surfaces
2. If unresolved in 24 hours, Scrum Master makes a binding call
3. If the Scrum Master is the party in conflict, majority vote decides

## Escalation — Zero Activity Rule
- Any member with **0 visible activity for 2+ consecutive days** is flagged by the Scrum Master
- Scrum Master sends a direct message and logs the flag in the team channel
- If no response or activity within 24 hours, the instructor is notified
